03 / The short version

Accountant first.

Full timeline

I studied Accounting at the University of Lagos, spent a season inside a bank’s financial control division, and then went to work on a payments API. Somewhere in there the two stopped being separate things.

Payments engineering is, underneath the HTTP, a bookkeeping problem. Every transfer is two entries, every retry risks a third, and correctness means the books still balance after the network has done its worst. I learned double-entry before I learned idempotency, and the second made immediate sense because of the first.
